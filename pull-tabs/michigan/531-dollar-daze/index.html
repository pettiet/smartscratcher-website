Quick answers

What does "pays back 68¢ per $1" mean?

It's the average return for every $1 played, counting every ticket still in the pool and every prize still unclaimed. Most tickets don't win, so any one ticket will land above or below it.

What are the odds of winning Dollar Daze?

The overall odds were 1 in 4.74 when the game started. That counts every prize, including the small ones.
