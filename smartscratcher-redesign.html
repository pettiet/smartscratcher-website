We can't promise wins — nobody can, it's a game of chance. What we can do is help you stop buying tickets for games where the big prizes are already gone. Better information means smarter decisions. Smarter decisions don't guarantee wins, but they definitely reduce losses.

Every day. Every active game. Every state we cover. The app always shows you when the data was last refreshed, so you know exactly how current it is.

Technically, yes — the raw data is public. But state lotteries only publish the "remaining prizes" count. They don't publish how many tickets are still out there, which is the critical piece for calculating real odds.

SmartScratcher estimates the remaining ticket count using the published odds and prize claims. That lets us show you the actual current odds for each tier, not just the ones printed at launch.

We use the published overall odds (provided by the lottery at launch) combined with the current number of remaining prizes. From those two numbers, we can work backwards to a reasonable estimate of tickets still in circulation.

With large prize pools and millions of tickets, the law of large numbers makes these estimates very reliable. It's the same kind of statistical reasoning casinos and actuaries use.
