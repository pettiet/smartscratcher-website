How we count

  • Everything on this page comes straight from official state lottery data, pulled fresh every morning.
  • "Weekly for life" and other installment prizes count at their full total payout — the whole prize, not just the first check.
  • Free-ticket prizes count at the ticket's shelf price.
  • "Unclaimed" means what each lottery currently reports as remaining. Big winners can take a while to come forward, so there's always a little lag baked in.
  • These numbers describe what's left on shelves — they don't predict what any one ticket will do. Nothing does. That's kind of the point of our app: play the games where the good prizes are actually still out there.
