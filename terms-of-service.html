1. What SmartScratcher is

SmartScratcher is an information and analysis tool for lottery scratch-off tickets. We gather the prize and odds figures that state lotteries publish, run our own calculations on them, and present the results as ratings, rankings, charts, daily write-ups, and alerts. Some features are free; others require a paid subscription.

The Services currently cover scratch-off games sold in California, Florida, Georgia, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Missouri, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Oklahoma, South Carolina, Texas, Virginia, and Washington. Coverage may differ between the App and the Website while a new state is being rolled out, and we may add or remove states at any time. The current list is always shown in the App and on the Website.

We are not a lottery and we do not sell tickets. SmartScratcher is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any state lottery or any lottery vendor. Lottery names, game names, logos, and ticket artwork belong to the respective lotteries and appear in the Services only to identify the games being discussed.

Everything in the Services is for general information only. In particular:

  • Our figures come from the lotteries. Prize counts, odds, and related details are taken from information the state lotteries publish. Lotteries update on their own schedules, sometimes lag, and sometimes contain errors. We may also make mistakes when collecting or calculating.
  • Ratings are opinions from math, not predictions. Our scores, rankings, "expected value," "ticket value," daily write-ups, and similar features are the output of our calculations applied to published figures.   • Scratch-off tickets are games of chance. Every ticket is random. Nothing in the Services changes the odds of any ticket, and past results do not affect future ones.
