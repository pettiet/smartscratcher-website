Quick answers

What is the best-selling scratch-off in Illinois?

By our count, $250,000 Crossword (a $10 ticket) is bringing in the most money: about 370,000 tickets a week over the last 4 weeks, roughly $3.7M a week, as of Nov 23, 2026. That is an estimate from prizes claimed times the game's overall odds.

Which Illinois scratcher sells the most tickets?

Holiday Ca$h moves the most tickets, about 420,000 a week at $1 each. $250,000 Crossword sells fewer tickets but brings in more dollars at $10 a ticket, which is why it ranks first above.

Is the best-selling game the best one to buy?

Not necessarily. Sales say what people are buying, not what is left on the board. A game can sell fast because of its name, its price, or because it just launched. The SmartScratcher app weighs the prizes still unclaimed against what is left to sell and scores every Illinois game 0 to 100 each morning, by our math.

How does SmartScratcher estimate sales?

The Illinois Lottery publishes how many prizes are still unclaimed at every prize level. The drop from one day to the next, times the game's overall odds, is our estimate of tickets sold. Prizes are counted when they are claimed, so a busy week shows up a few days late, and the numbers here are a pace, not a sales report.

Why are some Illinois scratch-offs missing from this ranking?

3 active games have less than three weeks of tracked sales, usually because they just launched. They will show up here after a few more weeks.

How fresh is this data?

Prize counts come straight from official Illinois lottery data, last refreshed on Nov 23, 2026. SmartScratcher updates every morning.
