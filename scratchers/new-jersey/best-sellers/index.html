Quick answers

What is the best-selling scratch-off in New Jersey?

By our count, $1,000,000 Payday (a $30 ticket) is bringing in the most money: about 150,000 tickets a week over the last 4 weeks, roughly $4.6M a week, as of Oct 31, 2026. That is an estimate from prizes claimed times the game's printed odds.

Which New Jersey scratcher sells the most tickets?

Crossword moves the most tickets, about 350,000 a week at $3 each. $1,000,000 Payday sells fewer tickets but brings in more dollars at $30 a ticket, which is why it ranks first above.

Is the best-selling game the best one to buy?

Not necessarily. Sales say what people are buying, not what is left on the board. A game can sell fast because of its name, its price, or because it just launched. The SmartScratcher app weighs the prizes still unclaimed against what is left to sell and scores every New Jersey game 0 to 100 each morning, by our math.

How does SmartScratcher estimate sales?

The New Jersey Lottery publishes how many prizes are still unclaimed at every prize level. The drop from one day to the next, times the overall odds printed on the ticket, is our estimate of tickets sold. Prizes are counted when they are claimed, so a busy week shows up a few days late, and the numbers here are a pace, not a sales report.

Why are some New Jersey scratch-offs missing from this ranking?

4 active games have less than three weeks of tracked sales, usually because they just launched. They will show up here after a few more weeks.

How fresh is this data?

Prize counts come straight from official New Jersey lottery data, last refreshed on Oct 31, 2026. SmartScratcher updates every morning.
