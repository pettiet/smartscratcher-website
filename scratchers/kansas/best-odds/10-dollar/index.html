What the printed odds don't say

Every number in the ranking above was printed before a single ticket was sold. From then on, tickets get scratched, prizes come off the board, and the odds on the back of the ticket never update to say so.

Right now every $10 Kansas game in this ranking still has at least one top prize left. That changes without notice, which is why the counts above are refreshed every morning.

Printed odds are half the story. SmartScratcher does the other half: it takes the prizes still on the board today and turns them into a ticket value, a 0–100 score, and a fresh ranking of every $10 Kansas game each morning — see today's real ranking in the app.
