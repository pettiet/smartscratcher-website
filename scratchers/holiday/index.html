Which holiday scratch-off has the best odds?

By the overall odds printed on the ticket, $3,000,000 Jingle Jumbo Bucks ($30, Georgia) at 1 in 2.54. Printed odds count every prize, including the ones that only give your money back, and they describe the game on the day it launched.

Are holiday scratch tickets a good gift?

They are a common one, for adults only: lottery tickets cannot be given to anyone under the legal age in your state. If you are picking one, the lists above show which games still have their big prizes. The SmartScratcher app goes further and scores each game from what is left, by our math.

When do holiday scratch-offs come out and go away?

Most lotteries put holiday games on sale in October or November and stop selling them after the new year. A game shows up here the first morning it appears in a state's official data and drops off when the lottery stops reporting it.
