Quick answers

What is the best-selling scratch-off in Washington?

By our count, $2,000,000 Diamond Dazzler (a $30 ticket) is bringing in the most money: about 53,000 tickets a week over the last 4 weeks, roughly $1.6M a week, as of Nov 14, 2026. That is an estimate from prizes claimed times the game's printed odds.

Which Washington scratcher sells the most tickets?

White Elephant moves the most tickets, about 120,000 a week at $1 each. $2,000,000 Diamond Dazzler sells fewer tickets but brings in more dollars at $30 a ticket, which is why it ranks first above.

Is the best-selling game the best one to buy?

Not necessarily. Sales say what people are buying, not what is left on the board. A game can sell fast because of its name, its price, or because it just launched. The SmartScratcher app weighs the prizes still unclaimed against what is left to sell and scores every Washington game 0 to 100 each morning, by our math.

How does SmartScratcher estimate sales?

The Washington Lottery publishes how many prizes are still unclaimed at every prize level. The drop from one day to the next, times the overall odds printed on the ticket, is our estimate of tickets sold. Prizes are counted when they are claimed, so a busy week shows up a few days late, and the numbers here are a pace, not a sales report.

Why are some Washington scratch tickets missing from this ranking?

5 active games have less than three weeks of tracked sales, usually because they just launched. They will show up here after a few more weeks.

How fresh is this data?

Prize counts come straight from official Washington lottery data, last refreshed on Nov 14, 2026. SmartScratcher updates every morning.
