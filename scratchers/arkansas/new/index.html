How do you know a Arkansas scratch-off is new?

SmartScratcher reads the official Arkansas lottery data every morning. A game counts as new on the first morning it appears there, so it can show up here a day or two after it reaches stores. We started tracking Arkansas on Oct 5, 2026; games already on sale that day are not listed as new.

Usually most of them. A game that launched this week has sold a small share of its tickets, so nearly every prize it started with is still out there — the top prizes left column on this page shows exactly how many. That changes as the game sells, which is why the counts are refreshed every morning.
