Does the Texas Lottery list remaining prizes?

The Texas Lottery's current games list shows how many prizes of each size were printed and how many have been claimed, but not how many are left, and it does not show odds. This page does the subtraction for every game, adds the printed overall odds, and refreshes every morning.

Are Texas scratch tickets the same as scratch-offs?

Yes. The Texas Lottery calls them scratch tickets; players also say scratch-offs or scratchers. They are the same games, and every one is on this page.

How many Texas scratchers have already lost their top prize?

1 of the 76 active games have had every top prize claimed — they're still on sale, but the jackpot printed on the ticket can't be won anymore. They're marked with a struck-through top prize in the tables above.
