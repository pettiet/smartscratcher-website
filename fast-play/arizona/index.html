What to look at before you pick

Quick answers

What is Arizona Fast Play?

Fast Play games are sold at Arizona Lottery retailers and print from the lottery terminal when you buy one. There's nothing to scratch and no drawing to wait for. The ticket shows right away whether it's a winner.

Is each ticket just random?

No. Each game is a set pool of tickets with a set number of prizes at every level, the same way a scratcher is. The lottery publishes how many prizes are left, and that is what these pages show.

Does every game have a jackpot that grows?

No. Some games have a progressive jackpot that grows as tickets sell and starts over when somebody wins it. The rest have a fixed top prize. The "Top prize" column marks which is which.

Do games share a jackpot?

No. In Arizona each progressive game has its own jackpot, fed only by its own ticket sales, and its own pool of tickets.

How often is this updated?

Twice a day, in the morning and again in the late afternoon. The jackpots keep growing in between, so the amount at the counter may be a little higher.

Is Fast Play in the SmartScratcher app?

Yes. Pick Arizona in the app and flip the switch at the top of the screen to Fast Play. The app adds a SmartScratcher Score, an estimated ticket value and a ranking for every game. This page shows the prize counts and jackpots.
