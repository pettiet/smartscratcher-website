Quick answers

What is the Ultimate Payday Progressive jackpot right now?

It was $679,572 at our last check, Oct 20, 2026 at 9:40 AM. It grows as tickets sell, so it may be a little higher at the counter.

What happens when somebody hits the jackpot?

The jackpot starts over and builds again. It belongs to Ultimate Payday Progressive alone, so no other game is affected.

What does "pays back 61¢ per $1" mean?

It's the average return for every $1 played, counting every ticket still in the pool and every prize still unclaimed. Most tickets don't win, so any one ticket will land above or below it.
