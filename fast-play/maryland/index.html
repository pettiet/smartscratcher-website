Maryland Fast Play:
what's still out there

Fast Play tickets print right at the counter. No scratching, you know on the spot. Behind the scenes each game is a set pool of tickets with a set number of prizes, just like a scratcher. We track what's left in every pool, prize by prize, twice a day. The app turns these counts into a score and a ranking for each game.

New to Fast Play? The jackpots work differently from scratchers, and the counts below show where each game stands. Read the 2-minute guide →

What to look at before you pick

  1. Does the game have a jackpot that grows? Some games do and some have a fixed top prize. Each jackpot belongs to one game.
  2. How many top prizes are left? Each game starts with a set number. Once they are won, that prize is gone from that game.
  3. How many prizes are left at every level? Each game's page has the full table: what it started with and what is still out there.
  4. Which one is worth it? That is the app's job: a SmartScratcher Score, an estimated ticket value and a ranking for every game, under the Fast Play switch.

Quick answers

What is Maryland Fast Play?

Fast Play games are sold at Maryland Lottery retailers and print from the lottery terminal when you buy one. There's nothing to scratch and no drawing to wait for. The ticket shows right away whether it's a winner.

Is each ticket just random?

No. Each game is a set pool of tickets with a set number of prizes at every level, the same way a scratcher is. The lottery publishes how many prizes are left, and that is what these pages show.

Does every game have a jackpot that grows?

No. Some games have a progressive jackpot that grows as tickets sell and starts over when somebody wins it. The rest have a fixed top prize. The "Top prize" column marks which is which.

Do games share a jackpot?

No. In Maryland each progressive game has its own jackpot, fed only by its own ticket sales, and its own pool of tickets.

How often is this updated?

Twice a day, in the morning and again in the late afternoon. The jackpots keep growing in between, so the amount at the counter may be a little higher.

Is Fast Play in the SmartScratcher app?

Yes. Pick Maryland in the app and flip the switch at the top of the screen to Fast Play. The app adds a SmartScratcher Score, an estimated ticket value and a ranking for every game. This page shows the prize counts and jackpots.
