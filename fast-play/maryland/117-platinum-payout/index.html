Quick answers

What is the Platinum Payout jackpot right now?

It was $108,056 at our last check, Oct 21, 2026 at 9:40 AM. It grows as tickets sell, so it may be a little higher at the counter.

What happens when somebody hits the jackpot?

The jackpot starts over and builds again. It belongs to Platinum Payout alone, so no other game is affected.

What does "pays back 64¢ per $1" mean?

It's the average return for every $1 played, counting every ticket still in the pool and every prize still unclaimed. Most tickets don't win, so any one ticket will land above or below it.

The overall odds were 1 in 3.62 when the game started. That counts every prize, including the small ones.
