Quick answers

What is the World Championship Poker® jackpot right now?

It was $165,368 at our last check, Oct 12, 2026 at 9:40 AM. It grows as tickets sell, so it may be a little higher at the counter.

What happens when somebody hits the jackpot?

The jackpot starts over and builds again. It belongs to World Championship Poker® alone, so no other game is affected.

What does "pays back 65¢ per $1" mean?

It's the average return for every $1 played, counting every ticket still in the pool and every prize still unclaimed. Most tickets don't win, so any one ticket will land above or below it.
