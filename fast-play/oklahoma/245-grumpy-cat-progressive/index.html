Best $2 game for the jackpot

On average, Grumpy Cat® Progressive pays back about 60¢ for every $1 played. The jackpot accounts for about a quarter of that average. The regular prizes alone pay back about 46¢ per $1. That gives it a score of 39 out of 100, which rates Poor.

The $2 jackpot that Grumpy Cat® Progressive plays for is $31,252. That is about four times its starting amount of $8,000. It grows by about 8¢ for every ticket sold on any of the three $2 games. Quick answers

What is the Grumpy Cat® Progressive jackpot right now?

It was $31,252 at our last check, Oct 1, 2026 at 4:00 PM. It grows as tickets sell, so it may be a little higher at the counter.

What happens when somebody hits the jackpot?

The jackpot starts over and builds again. It's shared by every $2 Fast Play game, so it resets for all of them.

What does "pays back 60¢ per $1" mean?

It's the average return for every $1 played, counting every ticket still in the pool and every prize still unclaimed. Most tickets don't win, so any one ticket will land above or below it.

What are the odds of winning Grumpy Cat® Progressive?

The overall odds were 1 in 3.48 when the game started. That counts every prize, including the small ones.
