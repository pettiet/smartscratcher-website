What to look at before you pick

  1. How big is the jackpot? Every game at the same price shares one jackpot. It grows as tickets sell and starts over when somebody hits it.
  2. Does the game still have jackpot tickets? Each game only has a few. Once they are won, that game cannot win the jackpot anymore. The "Jackpot tickets left" column shows where each game stands.
  3. How many prizes are left at every level? Each game's page has the full table, jackpot included.
  4. Which one is worth it? That is the app's job: a SmartScratcher Score, an estimated ticket value and a ranking for every game, under the Fast Play switch.

Quick answers

What is Oklahoma Fast Play?

Fast Play games are sold at Oklahoma Lottery retailers and print from the lottery terminal when you buy one. There's nothing to scratch and no drawing to wait for. The ticket shows right away whether it's a winner.

Is each ticket just random?

No. Each game is a set pool of tickets with a set number of prizes at every level, the same way a scratcher is. The lottery publishes how many prizes are left, and that is what these pages show.

Why do games at the same price show the same jackpot?

They share it. Every Fast Play game at one price feeds a single jackpot that grows as tickets sell. When somebody hits it, it starts over and builds again.

Can every game sharing a jackpot still win it?

Only while it has jackpot tickets left. Each game has its own pool of tickets with a few jackpot tickets inside it. The "Jackpot tickets left" column shows where each game stands today.

How often is this updated?

Twice a day, in the morning and again in the late afternoon. The jackpots keep growing in between, so the amount at the counter may be a little higher.

Yes. Pick Oklahoma in the app and flip the switch at the top of the screen to Fast Play. The app adds a SmartScratcher Score, an estimated ticket value and a ranking for every game. This page shows the prize counts and jackpots.
