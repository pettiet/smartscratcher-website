Quick answers

What is the Cash Blast Progressive jackpot right now?

It was $163,860 at our last check, Sep 29, 2026 at 10:48 AM. It grows as tickets sell, so it may be a little higher at the counter.

What happens when somebody hits the jackpot?

The jackpot starts over and builds again. It's shared by every $20 Fast Play game, so it resets for all of them.

What does "pays back 64¢ per $1" mean?

It's the average return for every $1 played, counting every ticket still in the pool and every prize still unclaimed. Most tickets don't win, so any one ticket will land above or below it.
