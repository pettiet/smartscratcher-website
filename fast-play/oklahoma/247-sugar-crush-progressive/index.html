This game depends on its jackpot

About half of the value in Sugar Crush Progressive is tied up in the jackpot. Only a few tickets can win it, so most tickets pay from the regular prizes.

On average, Sugar Crush Progressive pays back about 73¢ for every $1 played. Counting only the regular prizes, the game pays back about 39¢ per $1. That gives it a score of 66 out of 100, which rates Fair.

The $1 jackpot that Sugar Crush Progressive plays for is $27,928. That is about seven times its starting amount of $4,000. It grows by a nickel for every ticket sold on any of the three $1 games. When someone wins the jackpot, it starts over at $4,000. At that amount this game would pay back about 44¢ per $1.

Where each $1 goes

For every $1 played on Sugar Crush Progressive, about 73¢ comes back as prizes on average. The lottery keeps the other 27¢.

Quick answers

What is the Sugar Crush Progressive jackpot right now?

It was $27,928 at our last check, Oct 2, 2026 at 9:35 AM. It grows as tickets sell, so it may be a little higher at the counter.

What happens when somebody hits the jackpot?

The jackpot starts over and builds again. It's shared by every $1 Fast Play game, so it resets for all of them.

What does "pays back 73¢ per $1" mean?

It's the average return for every $1 played, counting every ticket still in the pool and every prize still unclaimed. Most tickets don't win, so any one ticket will land above or below it.

What are the odds of winning Sugar Crush Progressive?

The overall odds were 1 in 4.1 when the game started. That counts every prize, including the small ones.
