Same jackpot, better odds

Of the three games that share the $1 jackpot, Blazin' Bucks Progressive gives the best odds of winning it. The odds are about 1 in 73,000. That is about twice as good as the odds on Double Play Progressive.

Blazin' Bucks Progressive pays back about 77¢ for every $1 played, on average. The jackpot accounts for about half of that average. The regular prizes alone pay back about 39¢ per $1. That gives it a score of 73 out of 100, which rates Good.

The $1 jackpot that Blazin' Bucks Progressive plays for is $27,749. That is about seven times its starting amount of $4,000. It grows by a nickel for every ticket sold on any of the three $1 games. If the jackpot were won today, it would start over at $4,000. This game would then pay back about 44¢ per $1.

Where each $1 goes

For every $1 played on Blazin' Bucks Progressive, about 77¢ comes back as prizes on average. The lottery keeps the other 23¢.

Quick answers

What is the Blazin' Bucks Progressive jackpot right now?

It was $27,749 at our last check, Sep 30, 2026 at 4:00 PM. It grows as tickets sell, so it may be a little higher at the counter.

What happens when somebody hits the jackpot?

The jackpot starts over and builds again. It's shared by every $1 Fast Play game, so it resets for all of them.

What does "pays back 77¢ per $1" mean?

It's the average return for every $1 played, counting every ticket still in the pool and every prize still unclaimed. Most tickets don't win, so any one ticket will land above or below it.

What are the odds of winning Blazin' Bucks Progressive?

The overall odds were 1 in 3.89 when the game started. That counts every prize, including the small ones.
