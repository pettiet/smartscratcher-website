Quick answers

What is the Crossword Progressive jackpot right now?

It was $23,041 at our last check, Oct 10, 2026 at 4:00 PM. It grows as tickets sell, so it may be a little higher at the counter.

What happens when somebody hits the jackpot?

The jackpot starts over and builds again. It's shared by every $3 Fast Play game, so it resets for all of them.

What does "pays back 54¢ per $1" mean?

It's the average return for every $1 played, counting every ticket still in the pool and every prize still unclaimed. Most tickets don't win, so any one ticket will land above or below it.

What are the odds of winning Crossword Progressive?

The overall odds were 1 in 3.23 when the game started. That counts every prize, including the small ones.
