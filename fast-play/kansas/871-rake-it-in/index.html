Quick answers

What does "pays back 70¢ per $1" mean?

It's the average return for every $1 played, counting every ticket still in the pool and every prize still unclaimed. Most tickets don't win, so any one ticket will land above or below it.

What are the odds of winning Rake It In?

The overall odds were 1 in 3.35 when the game started. That counts every prize, including the small ones.
