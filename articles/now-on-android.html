The new math

From 15 → 19

This summer SmartScratcher went from nine states to fifteen in one jump. Today it goes to nineteen, and for the first time it runs on Android phones too.

The four new ones are Arkansas, Illinois, Kansas and Ohio. Each is tracked the same way as the rest: every active scratch-off, every prize level, refreshed whenever the state lottery posts new numbers, with a SmartScratcher Score and a short daily note per game.
