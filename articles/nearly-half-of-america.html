Meet the new ones

Three states. Three very different shelves.

01

Massachusetts

MA · Massachusetts State Lottery

The scratcher capital. Massachusetts is famous for being the heaviest lottery-playing state in the country per person, and the scratch-off shelf shows it — a deep lineup from $1 all the way up to the big-ticket $50 games. When a state plays this much, knowing which games are picked clean and which still have their top prizes actually matters.

Curious before you download? Every Massachusetts game's remaining prizes are free to browse.

Georgia

GA · Georgia Lottery

The biggest shelf we've ever tracked. Georgia runs nearly 200 active scratch-off games at once — more than any other state in the app. That's a lot of tickets to sort through at the counter, which is exactly the kind of problem the SmartScratcher Score was built for. (Bonus: Georgia Lottery money funds the HOPE Scholarship and pre-K programs, so the state takes its lottery seriously.)

Take a look first: every active Georgia game and what's left in it.

New Jersey

NJ · New Jersey Lottery

Boardwalk energy. New Jersey has been running a state lottery since 1970 — one of the oldest in the country — and the scratch-off lineup is big, busy, and full of games that share names with their own reprints. Sorting the fresh printing from the one that's been on the shelf all summer is exactly the kind of thing the app is good at.

Peek at every New Jersey game's remaining prizes for free.
