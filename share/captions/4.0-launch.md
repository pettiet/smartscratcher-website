## House rules baked into these
- No "guaranteed," "winning system," "beat the lottery," "hot," "due," or "lucky."
- Opinions about game value are always framed as ours ("by our math," "in our rankings").
- Fictional game names only (Dino Dollars, Corn Dog Cash — checked against all nine states' live rosters).
- Every article page carries the 18+/no-guarantee/1-800-GAMBLER footer.
